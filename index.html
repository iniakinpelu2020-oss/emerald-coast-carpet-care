03 How a visit runs

Same three steps, every room.

  1. 1

    Walk it with you

    We look at the traffic lanes, the pet spots and anything that has been spot-cleaned before.

  2. 2

    Pre-treat, then extract

    Edges and lanes get pre-spray, then hot-water extraction pulls the soil back out.

  3. 3

    Air movers, then a check

    Air movers bring it down to dry in four to six hours. You walk it with us before we pack up.
